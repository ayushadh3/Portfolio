About Aayush

Aayush Adhikari is a UK-affiliated BSc (Hons) Information Technology graduate who chose to build at home — pairing enterprise software discipline with the grounded pragmatism of Nepal's hills. From his base in New Baneshwor, Kathmandu, he leads Four G. Engineering Solution Pvt. Ltd., delivering IT, telecom and civil engineering solutions for businesses and municipal governments.

His GovTech work puts working software into public hands: Digital Citizen Charter systems, e-Building Permit portals and Digital Profiling applications that cut queues, paperwork and corruption surface area for local governments — engineered with Laravel, MySQL and Node.js for reliability in production government environments.

The same systems thinking runs uphill. In Dordi-1, Lamjung, he founded DORDI 346° — specialty Himalayan coffee and organic Aparajita (blue tea) — alongside MOMOSTE, a commercial frozen food brand, proving that world-class products can grow from village terraces.

"Software for the municipality, coffee from the hillside — both are infrastructure for a better Nepal."
