Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website (aayushadhikari.com.np) is the personal portfolio of Aayush Adhikari — Founder & Director of Four G. Engineering Solution Pvt. Ltd. and founder of DORDI 346°, operating from Kathmandu and Lamjung, Nepal ("I", "me", "my"). I am the data controller for information collected through this site. You can reach me at any time at hello@aayushadhikari.com.np.

2. What data I collect

  • Contact form data — when you use the contact form I receive the name, email address, project type and message you provide. Nothing more is requested.
  • Aggregate analytics data — if, and only if, you accept the cookie banner, a privacy-respecting analytics service (Plausible) records anonymous page-view statistics such as page URL, referrer, device type and approximate country. Plausible does not use cookies, does not fingerprint devices and does not identify individuals.
  • Server logs — the hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard, short-lived request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested URL) for security and abuse prevention, processed as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

I do not collect advertising identifiers, sell data, or use cross-site trackers of any kind.

3. How your data is used

  • Contact form submissions are used exclusively to respond to your inquiry. They are delivered to my project email via a transactional email service (Resend) and retained for up to 24 months, after which they are deleted.
  • Aggregate analytics data is used only to understand which pages are useful, so I can improve the site. It is never used to profile or advertise to you.
  • Server logs are used only for uptime, debugging and protection against spam and abuse.

4. Cookies & analytics consent

On your first visit you are shown a consent banner with Accept and Decline options. Your choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key aa-cookie-consent.

  • Decline — no analytics script is loaded at all; the site works fully without it.
  • Accept — the Plausible analytics script loads and records anonymous, cookie-free page views.
  • You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain; the banner will reappear on your next visit.

5. Who I share data with

  • Vercel Inc. — static hosting and serverless form processing (see Vercel's privacy policy).
  • Resend — delivery of contact form emails to my inbox (see Resend's privacy policy).
  • Plausible Analytics — only if you consent; anonymous, cookie-free statistics (see Plausible's privacy policy).

No other third parties receive your data, and none of the above is permitted to use it for advertising.

6. Your rights

Where GDPR or similar laws apply to you, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Nepal's Individual Privacy Act 2075 (2018) grants comparable rights to residents of Nepal. To exercise any right, email hello@aayushadhikari.com.np — I respond within 30 days.

7. Security

All traffic is served exclusively over HTTPS with modern TLS and Strict-Transport-Security enforced. The contact endpoint validates and sanitizes every submission server-side and applies rate limiting. No payment or sensitive identity data passes through this site.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
